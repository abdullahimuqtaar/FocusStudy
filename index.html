      Study tips

      Small habits that make your study time count.

      Active recall

      Close your notes and write or say what you remember. The effort of pulling it from memory is what makes it stick.

      Spaced repetition

      Review a topic again after a day, then three days, then a week. Spreading reviews out beats cramming the same hours into one night.

      Practice questions

      Test yourself with textbook questions or past papers. Every mistake shows you exactly what to study next.

      Take short breaks

      Step away between sessions. Stretch, drink water and look at something far away so your focus can recover.

      Remove distractions

      Put your phone in another room and close extra tabs before the timer starts. It is easier than resisting them mid-session.

      Write summaries

      After a session, summarize the main ideas in a few lines of your own words. If you get stuck, that is the part to revisit.

      Teach the topic to yourself

      Explain it out loud as if you were teaching a friend. Gaps in your explanation point to gaps in your understanding.
